# Sample-Inspired Product Listing & SEO Ranking Master Guide

> **Universal E-Commerce Standard for Multi-Brand Stores (TotVogue, Zaynahs, MiniMahal, LittleMister, etc.)**
> Zero Hardcoding: All brand names, domains, and categories are dynamically resolved at runtime.

## 🎯 1. Master Product Title Formula (&le;60 Characters)

Google displays the first 50–60 characters of a title tag on desktop and mobile SERPs. To capture maximum click-through rates (CTR) and high-intent Pakistani search queries, every product title MUST adhere to this pattern:

```text
[Gender/Audience] [Product Type] [Fabric/Material] [Age/Size] – [Season/Benefit] | [Brand]
```

### Universal Formula Variations:
1. **Apparel / Clothing (Kids / Adults)**:
   - `Boys Fleece Tracksuit 2-12 Years – Winter | [Brand]`
   - `Girls Cord Set Cotton 1-10 Years – Summer | [Brand]`
   - `Kids Night Suit Jersey 2-12 Years – COD Pakistan | [Brand]`
2. **Jewelry / Accessories**:
   - `Women Gold Plated Zircon Necklace Set | Festive | [Brand]`
   - `Bridal Kundan Choker Set with Earrings – COD | [Brand]`
3. **Footwear / Bags**:
   - `Kids Non-Slip Sneaker Shoes 1-6 Years – Casual | [Brand]`

## 🏷️ 2. Collection / Category Page SEO Formula

Category and collection pages carry the highest ranking power for generic search terms (`kids clothes`, `boys tracksuits`, `fleece suit`).

### A. Category Title Formula:
```text
Buy [Keyword] Online in Pakistan | [Key Benefit] | [Brand]
```
- `Buy Kids Tracksuits Online in Pakistan | COD & Free Delivery | TotVogue.pk`
- `Boys Jeans & Cargo Trousers in Pakistan | New Winter Arrivals | TotVogue.pk`
- `Girls Formal & Party Wear Dresses in Pakistan | Best Prices | TotVogue.pk`

### B. Category Meta Description Formula (150–160 chars):
```text
[Keyword] in Pakistan. [Fabric/Quality]. Sizes [Age/Size]. ✔ Cash on Delivery ✔ Fast Nationwide Delivery ✔ Easy Exchange. Order online from [Brand]!
```

### C. Clean URL Slug Standard:
- Hyphenated, lower-case, short, no dates, no IDs:
  - `/category/kids-tracksuits`
  - `/category/boys-winter-collection`
  - `/category/girls-cord-sets`

## 🖼️ 3. Image SEO Standard

1. **File Naming Formula**:
   - `[gender]-[color]-[product-type]-[age]-[brand].webp`
   - *Example*: `boys-black-fleece-tracksuit-2-12y-totvogue.webp`
2. **Alt Text Formula**:
   - `[Gender] [Color] [Product Type] [Age Range] [Country]`
   - *Example*: `Boys black fleece tracksuit 2-12 years Pakistan`
3. **Format & Sizing Invariant**:
   - Strictly WebP format.
   - File size: **&le; 100KB** (maximum efficiency, zero Core Web Vitals penalty).
   - Dimensions: 800x800 or 1200x1200 square.

---

## 📝 4. Product Description Formula (150–300 Words)

1. **Paragraph 1 (The Hook & Keyword)**:
   - Open with the primary search keyword in the first sentence.
   - Mention the fabric (e.g. export-quality 320 GSM cotton fleece), fit, and soft-touch lining.
2. **Bullet Points (Product Features)**:
   - Fabric composition & GSM.
   - Stitching details (reinforced seams, ribbed cuffs, elasticated waistband with drawstrings).
   - Pockets & functionality (dual cargo pockets, kangaroo pouch).
3. **Size & Age Guide**:
   - Clear measurements in inches (Chest, Length, Waist, Outseam).
4. **Occasion & Seasonality**:
   - Ideal for winter wear, daily outdoor play, travel, casual lounging.
5. **Trust & Shipping Guarantee**:
   - Cash on Delivery available all over Pakistan.
   - Dispatch within 24 hours, delivery in 2–4 working days.
   - 7-day hassle-free replacement guarantee.
